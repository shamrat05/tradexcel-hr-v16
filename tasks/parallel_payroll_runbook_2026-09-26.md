# Parallel payroll — 2026-09-26

**Not executed. Requires approved configuration and separate HR/Finance authorization.**

Use a fixed period and immutable roster/effective pay-authority snapshot. Review
Monthly and Daily Wage samples, joins/exits/transfers, leave/holidays, attendance
corrections, OT, arrears, benefits only where approved, and bank/cash/withheld pay.
Compare independent approved payroll against native slips component by component,
then employee totals, company totals, payable/expense accrual, payment voucher and
bank reconciliation. Classify every difference; never change historical submitted
slips to force parity. Use native reviewed correction/arrear/reversal paths.

Maker prepares; independent approver signs; Finance signs accounts/payment; HR
signs roster/work evidence. Record acceptance tolerance explicitly at native
currency precision. No live payment is implied by a parallel payroll test.
Unexplained difference, stale finalization, missing authority or legal scope
blocks cutover. Preserve reports, approvals and exact release/config versions.
