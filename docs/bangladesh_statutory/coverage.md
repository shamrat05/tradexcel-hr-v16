## Calculation and evidence boundaries

Unreviewed Form 80/81 observations are explicitly partial: current Company/Branch is only candidate scope, historical transfers remain findings, current status does not exclude dated leavers, missing Attendance is not absence, and no leaving reason is invented. A submitted employer return takes precedence; an existing draft prevents an automatic snapshot from bypassing work in progress.

The opt-in calculation requires independently reviewed establishment membership/classification, a private supporting original, a complete date-by-date work/holiday calendar and a completeness attestation. Every native employee working day needs unambiguous submitted Attendance. Worker/date observations are deduplicated; conflicting shifts, overlapping membership, missing dates or observations block calculation. Contractor/external observations need an explicit identity and evidenced whole workday count. Person-days divided by reviewed establishment working dates produces the disclosed draft averages. Native joining/relieving dates constrain membership; they never invent historical transfers or legal categories. Calendar/membership/Attendance counts, method and Attendance digest are recorded in calculation provenance. Unresolved draft recalculation clears stale derived cells.

Typed cells reject non-finite/negative counts or money, fractional integer counts and invalid dates. Prescribed totals reconcile with their parts. Service and withdrawal dates preserve source chronology. Blank/unresolved does not become zero. Populated unresolved facts cannot be submitted on any form. Evidence-only entries can independently review a private original without retyping professional determinations. Non-applicability requires an explicit reviewed reason. Service entries remain chronological; multiple conflicting active versions fail closed. Distinct employers or events on the same date remain separate natural history identities.

Financial evidence keeps calculated/submitted wages, payment instructed, reconciled, worker payment evidenced and acknowledgement separate. Each reviewed rate interval matches its native overtime dates/hours and native currency precision; aggregate amounts reconcile with unambiguous submitted provenance-linked Additional Salary. Mixed rates remain individual rows and never become a blended rate. Actual receipt amounts cannot exceed linked submitted net wages or be counted twice. Supporting private originals and portraits have submission-time SHA-256 fingerprints; replaced content is rejected. Portraits must fully decode as PNG/JPEG, rather than merely matching a file header. A zero-pay acknowledgement requires a genuinely zero-net submitted slip; it cannot create a zero payment claim. Native payroll/accounting records are never modified.

## Export and permission boundaries

Company/Branch/Employee/Department access is enforced through native permissions. Worker-specific manual registers can inherit an authorized Employee; establishment-wide returns cannot be exposed through a narrowed employee/department scope. Both downloads recheck permissions and produce immutable actor/layout/scope/source/file-hash audits. Word contains editable tables and native Company PNG/JPEG logo; readable native Letter Head and reviewed worker portraits remain optional. Wide tables are segmented without dropping fields. Signature boxes are unsigned working-copy placeholders.

All 31 generatable entries have historical four-format A4 checks. The additional four evidence-only entries intentionally have no generated PDF/DOCX. Actual native wkhtmltopdf and LibreOffice outputs were independently checked for A4 dimensions, text bounds, complete final rows and Bengali fonts; selected first/middle/last pages were optically inspected. Page counts above describe those earlier synthetic A4 inputs, not a statutory requirement for every employer. The 255-page Form 9 Word stress output has 85 deliberately differing worker-header groups. A separate coherent single-worker fixture with 85 leave entries rendered in 9 English / 8 Bangla A4 pages with the final row retained. Its Bangla extractor reordered the Latin marker fragments; optical inspection confirmed the intact displayed identifier.

The later configurable-page correction adds a practical two-sided **A7 Card** default for Form 6 and bounded per-export paper/custom-size, margin, logo-position, style and text-watermark choices. The A7 size is an engineering print preset, not a certified legal size. Other forms retain A4 by default; changing the paper does not certify layout fidelity on that size. The selected settings are audited, and physically unusual or long-content outputs need a test print before use.

## External gates and remaining acceptance

- Qualified current-law/2022/2026 applicability and translation approval; no template is automatically certified.
- The original Rule 353 machinery-layout source, including its precise document identity, remains unavailable.
- Genuine employer classifications, historical membership/transfer records, service and wage history, examinations, welfare/committee/fund particulars, receipt evidence and signatures must come from the employer or authorized external issuers.
- Real HR/Finance/legal acceptance and filing-authority acceptance remain external operational gates.

Fresh installation, backed-up migration and final full-suite evidence are recorded in the dated implementation note. Passing tests and a complete input workflow do not establish legal acceptance of a working-copy layout.
